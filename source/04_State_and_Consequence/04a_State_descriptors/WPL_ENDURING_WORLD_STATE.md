## Purpose

[[WPL_ENDURING_WORLD_STATE]] represents the persistent downstream world condition created by [[WPL_OWNERSHIP]] after the Wards Point Library mission is resolved.

It does not represent the initial handover itself.

The winning faction receives the library through the first successful report / key handover in [[WPL_07_DEBRIEF]]. This state makes the resulting owner-specific consequence path available for later world-state, environmental, narrative and gameplay consumers.

The transformation of Wards Point should develop resources and possibilities already established during the mission rather than replace the library with an unrelated faction-specific outcome.

## Peacekeeper Ownership

If [[WPL_OWNERSHIP]] = [PK], Wards Point develops toward an organized civic-security and continuity asset.

Potential enduring benefits include:

- systematic preservation and cataloguing of the municipal and technical archive;
- controlled use of technical knowledge for infrastructure repair and restoration;
- training of guards, repair personnel, first-aid capability and replacements;
- organized stores and resource accounting;
- accommodation and protected space for vulnerable civilians;
- increased local security;
- formal administration and planning;
- use of the library as a strategic base / continuity hub.

The same structure carries an enduring cost.

Access to important knowledge and resources becomes increasingly controlled. Public, communal and cultural uses may become subordinate to operational priorities. Responsibility can harden into permanent authority, formalized duty and increasingly compulsory obligation.

The Peacekeeper outcome should preserve the logic established in [[03_Factions_and_Characters]]:

**Chaos abandons the weak.**

Its long-term danger is that the institution created to protect people increasingly decides that only it is qualified to decide for them.

## Bastion Ownership

If [[WPL_OWNERSHIP]] = [BASTION], Wards Point develops toward a communal survival and local-autonomy asset.

Potential enduring benefits include:

- communal living space;
- shelter for families;
- broad community access;
- transparent / shared stores;
- rotating responsibilities;
- informal cultural and educational use;
- direct access to surviving knowledge and technical material;
- future food production using the adaptable atrium;
- reduced dependence on centralized authority.

The same structure carries an enduring cost.

Technical archives may receive less systematic preservation. Immediate human needs may take priority over irreplaceable records or long-term infrastructure planning. Larger-scale coordination becomes harder as the community grows, and informal cliques, charismatic influence or hidden hierarchy may emerge without formal accountability.

The Bastion outcome should preserve the logic established in [[03_Factions_and_Characters]]:

**Concentrated authority eventually owns the weak.**

Its long-term danger is that rejecting formal authority does not eliminate power, scarcity or difficult decisions; it can make responsibility and power harder to identify and coordinate.

## Consequence Standard

Neither ownership state is the correct outcome.

Each enduring state should produce:

- a real civilian or community benefit;
- a real cost arising from the faction's own governing logic;
- visible changes to Wards Point grounded in assets encountered during the mission;
- continued use of the library's established knowledge, infrastructure and communal capacity;
- opportunities for later narrative or gameplay systems to consume the ownership result where production scope allows.

The same physical resources should remain recognizable under either owner.

The difference is primarily **how those resources are governed, preserved, distributed and used**.

## Established Environmental Antecedents

Later changes may draw from existing mission elements including:

- the municipal archive;
- technical reference material;
- generator and heating infrastructure;
- maintenance / repair spaces;
- public library and educational areas;
- community-use space;
- cafeteria facilities;
- the adaptable glass atrium;
- the secured main entrance.

Do not introduce a later faction benefit that has no plausible antecedent in the Wards Point location unless separately established by downstream design.

## Implementation Boundary

[[WPL_EVENT07_WIN_RESOLUTION]] makes the appropriate owner-specific enduring-world-state path available after ownership is committed.

Exact implementation remains downstream work.

This descriptor does not prescribe:

- when occupation becomes visible;
- exact environmental changes;
- exact NPC populations;
- exact restored infrastructure;
- exact food-production output;
- exact access restrictions;
- exact reputation values;
- the number or timing of later callbacks;
- campaign-wide consequences beyond Wards Point.

Those details may be authored by later narrative, gameplay, level-design or world-state systems while preserving the benefit / cost direction established here.
