## Status

Authored.

## Established Responsibility

Records which faction receives Wards Point through the first successful report and [[WPL_E01_KEY]] handover after [[WPL_SECURED]].

Ownership is unresolved before the first successful handover.

[[WPL_EVENT07_WIN_RESOLUTION]] sets ownership to the winning faction.

Ownership is awarded once and is irreversible within the Wards Point Library mission. Reporting to the losing faction afterwards does not transfer or reopen ownership.
