# WPL_EVENT06_ARRIVAL

Fire once when Eli exits the elevator / service shaft and enters the cafeteria kitchen in [[WPL_06_ATRIUM]].

Trigger [[WPL_06_ATRIUM#^WPL-06-B01|Eli's cafeteria bark]]. If immediate combat would obscure it, defer delivery until a clear moment, as specified by the scene.

The mandatory kitchen encounter is governed by the scene's Gameplay requirements. Its activation must not depend on completing the bark. This arrival Event neither awards [[WPL_BOSS_DEFEATED]] nor resets an already defeated boss.
