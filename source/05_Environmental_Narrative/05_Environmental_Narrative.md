# Wards Point Library — Environmental Narrative

## Core Rule

Every important later world change should develop potential the player could encounter in the initial library. Environment establishes possibility before faction ownership realizes one use of it.

## Evidence and Environmental Detail

Evidence is a broad authored world-object category. Objects may be observed, collected, used, cleared, resolved or otherwise interacted with. Their individual definitions specify the required behaviour.

- [[WPL_E01_KEY]] and [[WPL_E02_FLASHLIGHT]] are physical inventory objects with practical uses.
- The basement generator, boiler, municipal archive and technical references establish useful infrastructure and knowledge.
- [[WPL_E06_ATRIUM_DOOR_LOCK]] is a physical obstruction resolved through gameplay after boss defeat.

Environmental storytelling does not require every useful object to have a formal Evidence file. Signage, the maintenance workshop, community rooms and service clutter can carry meaning through ordinary traversal.

There is no universal investigation interaction, Survivor Sense step, lore pickup or mandatory collection checklist. Use the interaction appropriate to each object. Main-floor and basement layout/encounters give Eli the opportunity to encounter the relevant features; selected Events trigger short contextual barks without interrupting inappropriate combat moments.

## Main Floor and Civic Spaces — [[WPL_04_MAIN_FLOOR]]

Stacks, reading areas, educational/reference signage, community rooms and adjoining offices establish public usefulness, shelter capacity and the building's civic history. They do not assign those resources exclusively to a faction.

The blocked stairs make the visible atrium a deferred destination. Research & Archives signage motivates descent without an explanatory monologue.

## Basement — [[WPL_05_BASEMENT]]

[[WPL_E05_MUNICIPAL_ARCHIVE]] and [[WPL_E05_TECHNICAL_REFERENCE]] establish finite practical knowledge: plans, utility records, repair procedures and trade material. [[WPL_E05_GENERATOR]] and [[WPL_E05_BOILER]] make sustained occupation and infrastructure restoration plausible.

Maintenance and storage spaces reinforce that this was a functioning civic building. The archive represents capability that can be preserved, shared, controlled, neglected or lost. Both factions can use it; their governance differs.

## Kitchen and Atrium — [[WPL_06_ATRIUM]]

The commercial kitchen establishes food-preparation capacity while serving as the enclosed boss encounter. The cleared door obstruction separates combat from the atrium's quiet reveal.

The atrium contains cafeteria/public-use remnants, broad sightlines and adaptable space. Its light and scale support plausible future cultivation, shelter, training or community use. Do not specify former school horticulture, planters, irrigation remnants or evidence of earlier growing success.

Optional faction consultation follows the qualified atrium progression and rival-contact requirements. It interprets the resources already encountered; it does not replace the environmental experience or transfer ownership.

## Antecedents and Potential Later Changes

| Initial feature | Potential later use or consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal/technical archive | Preservation and repair capability; controlled or shared access; possible degradation or loss |
| Generator, boiler and maintenance spaces | Restored power, heat and practical support for occupation |
| Public library, community and educational spaces | Shelter, learning, administration or training |
| Adaptable glass atrium and cafeteria | Community use, food preparation or future cultivation |
| Front entrance secured by Eli | Later faction-specific access and security arrangements |

Exact transformations and their timing remain downstream design. Preserve the distinction between potential visible in the building and a completed faction outcome.

## Time of Day

Dark interiors remain infected shelter; exterior danger and internal concentrations vary with time and light. The atrium remains a quiet space untouched by infected, with daylight or night sky/city-light views as appropriate. Gameplay retains encounter balance; the authored clearance and mandatory-boss requirements still apply.
